## What this shows

- **Caching the handbook** did most of the work. Stage 2 is 7.0× cheaper than stage 1, because about 10,100 handbook tokens are read from the cache at $0.01/1M instead of $0.10/1M.
- **Short structured replies and low reasoning** cut output tokens by about 64% (180 → 65), with the same 6/6 answer on practice doc p08.
- **Now:** the cached handbook is $0.000104 of the $0.000137 per call. Trimming what the model needs from the handbook is the next biggest saving (the §9 product catalogue is 6,920 of its 10,058 tokens).
- **Accuracy:** the stages above use one clean practice document (p08). On all 12 practice documents (`output/practice_scores.md`), every document without planted instructions was correct. The 3 misses were all fake 'revised price' or 'corrected lead time' lines, which is the undefended baseline for the prompt-injection problems.
